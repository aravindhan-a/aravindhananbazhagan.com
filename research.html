Research

Who builds AI, and who it changes

At CSET I work on two connected questions: who designs the chips that power AI, and how AI is changing work. My interests run across technology, labor markets, education systems and governance.

CSET · AI chips

The semiconductor supply chain and its workforce

I analyze data on the AI chip supply chain and link chip companies to labor market data to understand the workforce that designs them.

CSET · AI and work

How AI is reshaping the labor market

With CSET's AI workforce team I study which kinds of work are most exposed to AI and what that means for workers and for policy.

Earlier work · Tamil Nadu

Counting every child

As Project Associate with the Tamil Nadu School Education Department, I worked on the Out of School Children initiative, supported by an app across 37,000 schools and about 120,000 volunteers. I am now writing about how the state classified and counted these children.
